As of November 2026, the most advanced large language model (LLM) available is **OpenAI's GPT‑5**. It surpasses its predecessors and competing models (such as Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8, Google Gemini 3 Pro, and Deepseek V2) on benchmark scores, reasoning depth, multimodal capabilities, context window size (up to 1 million tokens), and real‑time learning integration.

Key highlights of GPT‑5:
- **Scale:** Over 1 trillion parameters with a sparse‑mixing architecture that efficiently routes inference.
- **Multimodal:** Native support for text, image, audio, and video inputs with unified reasoning.
- **Context length:** Up to 1 M tokens, enabling extremely long‑form tasks.
- **Real‑time adaptation:** Continual learning from user feedback while preserving privacy.
- **Benchmarks:** State‑of‑the‑art performance on MMLU, HumanEval + , BIG‑Bench, and the latest multimodal reasoning suites.
- **Safety:** Advanced alignment techniques and interpretability tools.

While other models are highly competitive in specific niches, GPT‑5 is currently regarded as the most capable general‑purpose LLM.
